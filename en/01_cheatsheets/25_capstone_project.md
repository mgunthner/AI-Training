# 25 – Capstone: My Personal AI Workflow Assistant

**Definition:** The **capstone** is the final project of the training: a documented, personal AI assistant with an optimized prompt library, safety concept and theoretical agent design – portfolio-worthy, in 2–4 hours.

> **Remember:** Reading 24 topics creates knowledge. Building your own project creates skill.

## Key points
- **Prerequisite:** Final test passed – the capstone builds on all blocks.
- **Three phases:** Needs analysis (3 recurring tasks, prioritized) → prompt engineering (before/after, systematically tested) → safety & scaling (risk analysis, boundaries, agent-lite design).
- **Three deliverables:** Prompt library (10+ entries), agent-lite concept (theoretical), safety concept – plus a reflection note.
- **Grading:** Self-assessment with the capstone checklist, 24 points, pass mark 17. Safety criteria are hard (pass or 0).
- **Concept instead of code:** The agent stays a design – implementing it is the next stage after this training.
- **Duration:** 2–4 hours, spread across several days – one phase per day.

## Example
Person A (fictional, administrative role) picks the **weekly report** in phase 1 (45 min./week). In phase 2 she improves the baseline prompt "Turn my notes into a report" in two rounds: role ("You write short status reports for management"), context (project names, fixed structure), constraints (max. 1 page, numbers only from the notes). In the test, the improved prompt saves about 25 minutes per report – the entry goes into the library with before/after and a test note. In phase 3 she sketches her agent-lite concept "report assistant" with approval before every send.

The complete sample (including grading) is in the example portfolio: `06_capstone/example_portfolio`.

## Do / Don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Choose tasks you really repeat | Choose the flashiest or most complex task |
| Keep the before/after versions | Document only the finished prompts |
| Set boundaries and safety rules early | Paint safety on at the very end |
| Work with invented or anonymized data | Paste real customer data or records into prompts |

## Safety note
The capstone collects many of your prompts and workflows in one place. That makes the library itself something worth protecting: no sensitive content, secure storage, and before any new automation idea, run the risk analysis from phase 3 first.

## Mini checklist
- [ ] I know that the final test is the gate to the capstone.
- [ ] I know the three phases and the three deliverables.
- [ ] I know that I grade it myself (checklist, 17/24 points).
- [ ] I know that the agent stays a concept and nothing gets implemented.

## Exercise (5 min.)
Write down three recurring tasks from your everyday life and estimate for each: how often per week? How many minutes does it cost? Mark the one task you would take into your capstone first – and the reason.
