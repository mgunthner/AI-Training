# Introduction: AI Training for Beginners

## 1. Welcome and goal
You already use AI in everyday life or want to get started with it. In this training you will learn how to use AI **safely, sensibly and with cost awareness**. After the training you will be able to:
- explain what an AI chatbot can and cannot do
- write requests (prompts) that deliver usable results
- recognize wrong answers and manipulation attempts
- decide which data belongs in a chat and which does not
- understand what agents, interfaces (APIs) and the like are

## 2. Who is this training for?
For everyone without prior knowledge. All you need is a device with internet access and access to any AI service – a free plan is enough. The training is vendor-neutral: everything works with any common service (e.g. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot).

## 3. How the training works
You learn by self-study, at your own pace. Per topic (about 25–30 min.):
1. **Read the cheatsheet** (about 10 min.)
2. **Do the exercise** (5–10 min.)
3. **Extra exercises** (optional)
4. **Questions?** Ask them as an issue in the GitHub repo
5. **Test** after each block

**Choose your learning path:**

| Path | Duration | For whom |
|---|---|---|
| Quick start | about 3 hrs. | everyone, mandatory part |
| Standard | about 1 day | regular users |
| Advanced | about 2 days | tech enthusiasts |

You can find the exact topics per path in the roadmap (`AI-Training_Roadmap`). You can always go deeper later.

## 5. Five safety rules up front
1. **Enter no passwords, keys or sensitive data** (also no other people's data).
2. **Check results** before you use or share them. "Sounds convincing" does not mean "is true".
3. **Do not blindly trust third-party content** (websites, documents, emails can contain hidden instructions).
4. **Confirm actions with consequences yourself** (sending, deleting, paying, publishing).
5. **Know your privacy settings**: Check what your service stores and whether your inputs are used for training.

## 6. Getting ready to start
- [ ] Create an account with an AI service of your choice
- [ ] Open and check the privacy settings (storage, use for training, history)
- [ ] Create your own **practice chat** (e.g. "AI training") to collect your exercises
- [ ] Create a note file for your **prompt library**

Tip: For exercises, use only **invented or anonymized data**, e.g. "Person A" or "Company X".

## 7. Tests and progress
The tests are not a report card but a mirror: they show you what has stuck and what you should review.
- **Entrance test** (10 questions): before you start, not graded. It shows your starting point. (`03_tests/entrance_test`)
- **Block tests:** after each block – Test A (`03_tests/test_A_foundation`), Test B (`03_tests/test_B_good_results`), Test C (`03_tests/test_C_risks`), Test D (`03_tests/test_D_technology_costs`), Test E (`03_tests/test_E_agentic_systems`), Test F (`03_tests/test_F_final`)
- **Quick test: quick start:** after the quick start path (`03_tests/quick_test_quickstart`)
- **Final test:** at the end, mixes knowledge and practice (`03_tests/final_test`)
- **Pass mark:** 75 %. You grade it yourself, using the solutions file. You assess practice tasks with the self-assessment rubric (`03_tests/self_assessment`) – criterion by criterion, stricter rather than lenient when in doubt. Only look at the solution when you are done.
- **Not passed?** The solution names the topics you should review. After that you can retake the test.
- **After that?** With the capstone (`06_capstone/project_instructions`) you turn what you have learned into your own project – optional in the Standard path, recommended in the Advanced path.

Tick off your progress in the checklist (read / practiced / test passed).

## 8. Extra exercises
For every topic there are 3–5 extra tasks in three levels: ★ entry, ★★ everyday, ★★★ challenge. Use them when you want to consolidate a topic or the regular exercise was too easy. Solution approaches are at the end of the file.

## 9. Asking questions (GitHub issues)
You can ask questions at any time, especially when you get stuck on an exercise. The way to do that is through **issues in this GitHub repo**:
- Open the repo and create a new issue under **Issues**.
- Copy the template from `04_trainer/question_template` into the issue and fill it in:
  - Name the **topic** (number of the cheatsheet).
  - Write what you **wanted to achieve** and what you **tried**.
  - Say **where you are stuck**.
- **Anonymize** prompts and results before you write them into the issue. No sensitive or personal data – issues are publicly visible.

Solved questions are collected anonymously in the FAQ.

> Note: If you do not have a GitHub account, you will need a one-time free account to be able to create an issue.

## 10. Learning tips
- Better small units than one long marathon.
- Apply every topic immediately to a real everyday task (email, vacation, shopping, learning).
- Take notes and collect successful prompts in your **prompt library**, from day 1.
- Experiment, compare results and question them.

## 11. Mini glossary
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AI** | Computer programs that solve tasks for which you would otherwise need human thinking |
| **LLM** | Language model: a program that understands and generates text by predicting the most likely next word |
| **Prompt** | Your input or instruction to the AI |
| **Token** | Small chunk of text in which the AI processes and bills text |
| **Context** | Everything the AI currently has "in front of it" in a chat (history, files, instructions) |
| **Hallucination** | An invented answer that sounds convincing |
| **Model** | The "engine" behind the chat; there are larger and smaller ones |
| **Plan** | Your plan with the provider (e.g. free or subscription) with its own limits |
| **API** | Interface through which programs talk to the AI directly |
| **Agent** | AI that independently plans multiple steps and executes them with tools |

## 12. Important notes
- Providers change features, limits and prices often. Always check current details with the provider.
- The content is general orientation, **not legal advice**. If in doubt, ask a specialist or your data protection officer.
- AI makes mistakes. You remain responsible for what you do with the results.
