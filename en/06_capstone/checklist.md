# Capstone checklist: Self-assessment

Grade your capstone project yourself – criterion by criterion, not by gut feeling. The procedure is the same as with the self-assessment rubric of the tests: check, award points (0 / 0.5 / full), add up at the end. **When in doubt, use the lower rating.**

**Total: 24 points · Pass mark: 17 points**

---

## 1. Tick off the phases

- [ ] Final test passed (75 %)
- [ ] Phase 1: 3 tasks identified and prioritized
- [ ] Phase 2: prompt library finished (10+ entries)
- [ ] Phase 3: safety concept + agent-lite concept + reflection note
