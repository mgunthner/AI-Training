## Topic 22 – Legal & Compliance

**Question 1 · Multiple choice · 1 point**
Which statement is definitely **personal data**?
- A) "The shop had 120 visitors on Monday."
- B) "Anna Sample, born 3 March 1985, living at 1 Example Street."
- C) "The average price of bread is 3.20."
- D) "It is raining in Sampletown."

**Question 2 · True/False with reasoning · 1 point**
"Because the AI wrote the newsletter text, the AI bears the responsibility if there's a mistake in it."
True or false? Give your reason in one sentence.

**Question 3 · Practical task · 2 points**
You want to have this made-up customer message analysed by an AI:

```
From: Sabine Schneider, 3 Sample Lane, 12345 Smalltown
Dear Team, I received a defective coffee machine on 5 March
(invoice 2026-4471). Since my knee surgery I have been very
sensitive, please help quickly. Tel.: 0123/456789
```

(a) Name at least three pieces of personal or especially sensitive information. (1 point)
(b) Write the anonymized version that is safe to paste into the chat. (1 point)

**Question 4 · Multiple choice · 1 point**
Your club (fictional) wants to publish a newsletter text that the AI formulated from your bullet points. What is the most sensible approach to labeling?
- A) Don't mention it at all – the AI is just a tool.
- B) Clarify with the members what the club requires; if in doubt, briefly indicate it (e.g. "created with AI support").
- C) Pass the text off as "100 % human-made".
- D) Don't use the text at all.

**Question 5 · True/False with reasoning · 1 point**
"For exams at my institution, what the AI service allows in its terms of service applies."
True or false? Give your reason in one sentence.

## Topic 23 – Everyday Workflows & Prompt Library

**Question 6 · Multiple choice · 1 point**
What belongs in a clean library entry?
- A) Title, purpose, the finished prompt, sample result (anonymized)
- B) Only the prompt – the rest is self-evident
- C) Your real customer data as an example
- D) The password of the AI service, so you don't forget it

**Question 7 · Practical task · 2 points**
Complete this library entry for **executive summaries**:

```
Title: Executive Summary
Purpose: ______________________
Prompt: ______________________
Sample result: ______________________
```

Graded: a sensible purpose (1 point) and a prompt with target audience and format specification (1 point).

**Question 8 · Scenario · 1 point**
Your library prompt has been delivering poor results for days (vague, long). What is the best next step?
- A) I delete the entry and hope the problem disappears.
- B) I rework the prompt (more concrete, format specification), test it and update the entry.
- C) I double the prompt length with filler words.
- D) I switch my AI service.

## Topic 24 – Glossary & Further Learning

**Question 9 · Practical task · 2 points**
Explain each of the following in **one sentence** for someone with no prior knowledge:
- (a) **Hallucination** (1 point)
- (b) **Human-in-the-loop** (1 point)

**Question 10 · Multiple choice (several correct) · 2 points**
What belongs to the **new-feature check** for new AI features? Select all correct ones.
- A) Benefit: Does it solve a real problem of mine?
- B) Risk: What data and accesses does it expose?
- C) Costs: What does it cost (money, limit, time)?
- D) Data privacy: Where do my inputs end up?
- E) Hype: Does everyone on social media have it right now?

*(2 points for a fully correct selection, 1 point for exactly one mistake, otherwise 0.)*

**Question 11 · Multiple choice · 1 point**
Which source is the most reliable for current features and limits of an AI service?
- A) A TikTok video from yesterday
- B) The provider's official documentation and help pages
- C) A chat with the AI itself
- D) A forum post from last year

**Question 12 · True/False with reasoning · 1 point**
"It's best to try out new AI features immediately with my real data – that's how I learn fastest."
True or false? Give your reason in one sentence.
