# Self-Assessment Rubric for Practical Tasks

You assess practical tasks ("Practical task", "Safety check", "Scenario with your own solution") yourself. To make this consistent and honest, use this rubric: you check your answer **criterion by criterion** – not by feel ("it roughly fit").

---

## 1. How to assess a practical task

1. Only after your answer, look at the solutions.
2. For each practical task, the solutions name the **assessment criteria** (a), (b) … with 1 point each.
3. Check each criterion individually:

| Rating | Points for the criterion |
|---|---|
| **Met** – your answer contains the criterion clearly and completely | full points |
| **Partially** – approach recognizable, but incomplete or unclear | half points |
| **Missing** – criterion not met or met incorrectly | 0 points |

4. Add up the points across all criteria of the task.
5. Pass mark: keep calculating in 0.5-point steps and check at the end whether you reach the mark. If in doubt, it helps: review again rather than just squeaking through.

**Rule of thumb:** If in doubt, choose the lower rating. The rubric is a mirror, not a favour-grading.

---

## 2. Worked example

**Task (2 points):** "Improve the prompt: `Make me a study plan.`
(a) Name the goal and context concretely. (1 point) (b) Specify the format and at least one constraint. (1 point)"

Your answer: `Create a study plan for my Spanish vocabulary, 20 minutes per day. Maximum 10 new words per day, as a table.`

| Criterion | Check | Rating |
|---|---|---|
| (a) Goal + context concrete | Goal (Spanish vocabulary) and context (20 min/day) named | 1 P |
| (b) Format + constraint | Format (table) and constraint (max. 10 words) named | 1 P |

**Result: 2 of 2 points.**

Had the answer only been: `Create a study plan for Spanish.` – then (a) would be partially met (goal yes, context missing = 0.5 P) and (b) missing (0 P).

---

## 3. Common self-deception traps

- **"But that's what I meant!" doesn't count.** Only what is actually written down fulfills a criterion.
- **Leaving requirements out of your head:** If the task asks for "at least two" and you name one, the criterion is partial (half points).
- **Safety criteria are hard:** If an approval or a least-privilege entry is missing in the safety check, the criterion gets no "partial" rating – it's either in there or it isn't.
- **Don't forget the reasoning:** For "True/False with reasoning", the reasoning counts too. Answering correctly without reasoning = half points.

---

## 4. What to do when you disagree with yourself?

If you are not sure whether you met a criterion, compare it with the model answer in the solutions file. If the model answer is framed differently from your answer, take the model answer as the standard. If the question itself is unclear or contradictory, raise it as an issue (see `04_trainer/question_template`) – that way we improve the question for everyone.
