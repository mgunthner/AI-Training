# Test A – Foundation (topics 01–04)

**Scope:** 19 questions · **Points:** 24 · **Pass mark 75 %:** 18 points

## How the test works
- The test shows you what has stuck and what you should review. It is not a certificate.
- Work without the cheatsheets. That way you see what you have really retained.
- Write down your answers (on paper or in a note file) before looking at the solutions.
- The solutions are separate: `03_tests/test_A_foundation_solutions`. Only look at them once you're done.
- You grade practical tasks and safety checks yourself using the self-assessment rubric (`self_assessment`) – criterion by criterion.
- No time limit. All examples contain only fictional data.

## Topics 01 – What is AI?

**Question 1 · Multiple choice · 1 point**
What describes a language model (LLM) best?
- A) A database of verified facts
- B) A program that produces text by predicting likely-fitting next pieces of text
- C) A digital human who thinks like you
- D) A search engine with its own archive

**Question 2 · True/False with reasoning · 1 point**
"If an AI answer sounds very convincing and detailed, it is very likely correct."
True or false? Explain in one sentence. (1 point only for the correct verdict plus reasoning.)

**Question 3 · Multiple choice (several correct) · 2 points**
Which tasks are typical strengths of a chatbot? Choose all correct answers.
- A) Rephrasing a text
- B) Summarizing a long email
- C) Reliably reporting current events without web search
- D) Delivering exact source references that you don't need to check
- E) Collecting ideas for a birthday party

*(2 points for a completely correct selection, 1 point for exactly one mistake, otherwise 0.)*

**Question 4 · Multiple choice · 1 point**
What, in principle, distinguishes a chatbot from a classic search engine?
- A) There is no difference.
- B) The chatbot automatically checks every statement against original sources.
- C) The chatbot formulates answers anew; the search engine lists existing webpages.
- D) The search engine can only find images.

**Question 5 · Scenario · 1 point**
You ask an AI without web search about the result of yesterday's game. It confidently names a result. What makes the most sense?
- A) You believe it, because it sounds confident.
- B) You ask the question several times and take the most frequent answer.
- C) You start a new chat and ask again.
- D) You turn on web search or check the result with a reliable source.

## Topic 03 – Model selection & free plans

You want a sentence in an email phrased more politely. Which approach fits best?
- A) The largest available model with maximum reasoning effort
- B) None at all, because AI can't write emails
- C) A smaller, faster model is usually enough
- D) A model that uses as many tokens as possible

- A) Access to certain models
- B) The amount of usage per period
- C) Some features
- D) The basic ability to write texts

*(2 points for a completely correct selection, 1 point for exactly one mistake, otherwise 0.)*

**Question 12 · True/False with reasoning · 1 point**
"The largest model is the best choice for every task."
True or false? Explain in one sentence.

**Question 13 · Scenario · 1 point**
You have an AI check an anonymized contract text for notice periods. The decision has financial consequences. What is sensible?
- A) Use a more powerful model and check the result against the original text
- B) Use the smallest model and adopt the answer unchecked
- C) Ask the same question five times and take the most frequent answer
- D) Adopt the answer if it sounds thorough

## Topic 04 – Data privacy in everyday life

**Question 15 · Multiple choice · 1 point**
Which input is the least problematic?
- A) A photo of your ID card so the AI can fill out a form
- B) A list with names and email addresses of your company's customers
- C) Your online banking password so the AI can help you log in
- D) A fictional email with "Person A" and "Company X" for practice

**Question 16 · Safety check · 2 points**
Find the mistakes in this prompt (all details fictional):

```
Write a friendly rejection letter to Mr. Anton Sample,
Main Street 1, 00000 Sampletown, who applied as an accountant at
Sample Ltd. His mobile number is 0000 000000. He mentioned that
he has diabetes.
```

(a) Name at least three details that do not belong in the chat. (1 point)
(b) Rewrite the prompt so that it is anonymized but still works. (1 point)

**Question 17 · True/False with reasoning · 1 point**
"When I upload a screenshot, the AI only sees what I deliberately wanted to show."
True or false? Explain in one sentence.

**Question 18 · Scenario · 1 point**
You want to summarize an internal document with customer data from your office using AI. You don't know whether your employer allows this. What do you do?
- A) You use your private account, because it's faster.
- B) You clarify it first with your superiors or the data protection officer and use the company account, if one exists.
- C) You only leave out the names, then everything is allowed.
- D) You upload it and delete the chat afterwards.

**Question 19 · Multiple choice · 1 point**
Which measure reduces the risk that your inputs are used for training?
- A) Turning off training use in the settings, if your service offers that
- B) Starting every message with the word "confidential"
- C) Renaming the chat
- D) Activating dark mode
