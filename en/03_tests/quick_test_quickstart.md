# Quick test: quick start (topics 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 08, 12)

**Scope:** 15 questions · **Points:** 20 · **Pass mark 75 %:** 15 points

## How the test works
- The test shows you what has stuck and what you should review. It is not a certificate.
- Work without the cheatsheets. That way you see what you have really retained.
- Write down your answers (on paper or in a note file) before looking at the solutions.
- The solutions are separate: `03_tests/quick_test_quickstart_solutions`. Only look at them once you're done.
- You grade practical tasks and safety checks yourself using the self-assessment rubric (`self_assessment`) – criterion by criterion.
- No time limit. All examples contain only fictional data.

## Topic 01 – What is AI?

**Question 1 · Multiple choice · 1 point**
What describes an LLM (Large Language Model) best?
- A) A database with all the facts in the world.
- B) A "text prediction machine" that predicts the next text based on patterns in training data.
- C) A search engine with the most up-to-date results.
- D) A program that always tells the truth.

**Question 2 · True/False with reasoning · 1 point**
"Sounding convincing does not mean it's true."
True or false? Explain in one sentence what this rule of thumb means for everyday use.

## Topic 02 – Tokens & context window

**Question 3 · Multiple choice · 1 point**
Your chat suddenly seems forgetful and ignores information from the beginning of the conversation. What is the most likely cause?
- A) The AI is tired.
- B) The long conversation history fills the context window; details from the beginning get "lost".
- C) You bought too few tokens.
- D) The AI deliberately stops storing anything.

**Question 4 · Scenario · 1 point**
Your chat has become very long over days. What is the most sensible next step?
- A) Keep writing and hope.
- B) Have the conversation summarized in 10 lines and continue in a new chat.
- C) Delete everything and start from scratch, without a note.
- D) Write the prompt in capital letters.

## Topic 04 – Data privacy in everyday life

**Question 7 · Multiple choice (several correct) · 2 points**
What does **not** belong in an AI chat? Choose all correct answers.
- A) Passwords and API keys
- B) Other people's health data
- C) A fictional sample task with "Person A"
- D) Bank details and ID data
- E) Confidential company and customer data

*(2 points for a completely correct selection, 1 point for exactly one mistake, otherwise 0.)*

**Question 8 · Scenario · 1 point**
You want an AI to check an email containing customer data. How do you proceed safely?
- A) I upload the email — the AI is discreet, after all.
- B) I anonymize names, places and license plates ("Person A", "Company X") and additionally check my service's privacy settings.
- C) I also type in my private phone number in case follow-up questions are needed.
- D) I send the data to three services and pick the best answer.

## Topic 05 – Prompt basics

**Question 9 · Multiple choice · 1 point**
Which prompt will most likely deliver the most usable result?
- A) `Write something nice.`
- B) `Write a short rejection for applicant Person A, factual, appreciative, at most 5 sentences. Reason: The position has already been filled.`
- C) `Application rejection Person A short.`

## Topic 12 – Prompt injection

**Question 13 · Multiple choice · 1 point**
What is **indirect** prompt injection?
- A) You deliberately type tricks into the chat to trick the AI.
- B) Hidden instructions in external content (document, webpage, email) that the AI processes.
- C) The AI makes up false facts on its own.
- D) A long-running chat loses details.

**Question 14 · Safety check · 2 points**
The AI, which has access to your (fictional) inbox, suddenly recommends opening an unknown link and "paying right away with it".
(a) Name two warning signs in this situation. (1 point)
(b) Describe your approach in at least two concrete steps. (1 point)

**Question 15 · True/False with reasoning · 1 point**
"Actions with consequences (sending, deleting, paying) I always confirm myself — technical safeguards do not replace that."
True or false? Explain in one sentence.
