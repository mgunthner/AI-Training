## Topic 05 – Prompt basics

**Question 1 · Multiple choice · 1 point**
You want an AI to write an invitation to a barbecue evening. Which prompt will most likely deliver the best result?
- A) `Write an invitation.`
- B) `Write an invitation to a barbecue evening on Saturday at 6 p.m. in my garden. Target audience: friends and neighbors. Tone: casual, informal "you". Length: at most 6 sentences. Please ask for RSVP by Wednesday.`
- C) `You are the world's best invitation writer. Write something great.`
- D) `Invitation barbecue garden people.`

**Question 2 · Practical task · 2 points**
Your prompt is: `Make me a study plan.`
Rewrite it so that it
- (a) states goal and context concretely and (1 point)
- (b) specifies format and at least one constraint. (1 point)

**Question 3 · True/False with reasoning · 1 point**
"The best way is to pack five different tasks into one single long prompt; then the AI works most thoroughly."
True or false? Explain in one sentence.

## Topic 06 – Prompt techniques

**Question 4 · Multiple choice · 1 point**

- A) You include one to three examples of the desired style or format.
- B) You ask the AI only a few questions per day.
- C) You limit the answer to a few words.
- D) You switch off features of the service.

**Question 5 · Multiple choice (several correct) · 2 points**
Which statements about prompt techniques are correct? Choose all correct answers.
- A) Separators like `### Text` help to separate your own text from the instructions.
- B) A role like "You are the world's best expert" makes the answer reliably correct.
- C) You can ask the AI to critically review its own answer, e.g., "Find 3 weaknesses in your answer."
- D) A positive instruction ("Write in short sentences") usually works better than only a prohibition ("Don't write long").
- E) Including examples never helps.

*(2 points for a completely correct selection, 1 point for exactly one mistake, otherwise 0.)*

**Question 6 · Practical task · 2 points**

Extend it with
- (a) an example of the desired style (few-shot) and (1 point)
- (b) a format specification. (1 point)

**Question 7 · Scenario · 1 point**
The AI's result is too long and too formal. What makes the most sense?
- A) You start a new chat and enter the same prompt again.
- B) You repeat the prompt in capital letters.
