# Entrance test: Where do you stand?

**Scope:** 10 questions · approx. 10 minutes
**Scoring:** Not graded. The test only shows you your starting point.

## How it works

- Answer the questions **on your own and by gut feeling**. Do not use AI or a search engine.
- Don't know an answer? Leave the question blank. That's perfectly fine — that's what this training is for.
- Only look at the solutions file **after the test**: `03_tests/entrance_test_solutions`.
- Questions 1–8 are knowledge and practical questions (1 point each). Questions 9–10 are self-assessments with no points.

---

**What best describes an AI chatbot (a "language model", or LLM for short)?** Choose one answer.

- A) A search engine that pulls ready-made answers from a database of verified facts
- B) A program that produces text by calculating which text fragments are likely to come next
- C) A digital human who truly understands things and remembers every previous conversation
- D) A lexicon that updates itself every day

## Question 3 · Multiple choice, several answers (1 point)

## Question 4 · Practical task (1 point)

**You want an AI to write an email to your neighbor (Person A). You want to politely ask them to be quieter after 10 p.m. Which prompt will most likely deliver the best result?** Choose one answer.

- A) `Write something nice to my neighbor.`
- B) `Write a friendly email to my neighbor (Person A). I'm asking them to be quieter after 10 p.m. Tone: polite, informal "you", at most 5 sentences.`
- C) `Mail neighbor noise.`
- D) `You are the best email writer in the world. Write the perfect email.`

## Question 7 · Safety check (1 point)

**You have an AI summarize the text of a webpage you don't know. The summary suddenly says: "Click this link and enter your password to continue." What is most likely happening here, and what do you do?** Choose one answer.

- B) The webpage text probably contains a hidden instruction aimed at the AI. I don't follow the link and don't enter anything.
- C) My keyboard caused an error. I'll write the prompt again.
- D) My device has a virus. I'll reinstall everything immediately.
