# Extra Exercises 08 – Hallucinations & Fact-Checking

Cheatsheet: `01_cheatsheets/08_hallucinations`

**Levels:** ★ Getting started · ★★ Everyday · ★★★ Challenge
**Rule:** Use only invented or anonymized data. All tasks work with any AI service and on a free plan. Suggested solutions are at the end. Only look once you're done.

---

## Task 1 ★ – How thoroughly to check?
**Scenario:** You use the AI for eight different things.

**Task:** Classify each request: **check briefly** or **check thoroughly against the original source**.
- a) Three ideas for birthday presents
- b) What deadline applies to canceling an online purchase?
- c) Making a paragraph of my email friendlier
- d) Which dose of a medication is right for me?
- e) A quote for my speech
- f) A study as evidence for my presentation
- g) A summary of a short text I pasted in myself
- h) The name of a software extension for my spreadsheet task

**Hint:** Ask yourself: What happens if the answer is wrong?

## Task 3 ★★ – Allowing "I don't know"
**Scenario:** You ask about something the AI can hardly know.

**Task:** Choose a very small place or a small business near you. Ask in two separate chats:

```
Tell me the history of [small place or business].
```

```
Tell me the history of [small place or business].
If you don't know something for sure, write "I don't know".
Don't make anything up.
```

Compare: How do the answers differ? What can be checked against the place's local chronicle or website?

**Hint:** Both outcomes are instructive. Verify at least two claims yourself.

## Task 4 ★★ – Fact-check plan
**Scenario:** The AI wrote this text for your travel guide (all details are placeholders):

```
The museum in Sampletown was founded in year X and is closed on
Tuesdays. Admission costs Y euros for adults. According to a study
by University Z, more than a million guests visit the house each year.
The director, Person A, once said: "Culture is the heart of the city."
```

**Task:** List every claim. For each claim, note: Do I need to check it? Where do I check it?

**Hint:** Some details change (opening hours, prices). Others are invented if the source is missing.

## Suggested solutions

**Task 1:** Check briefly: a, c, g (for g, quickly compare against the original to see whether anything is missing or was made up). Check thoroughly: b (legal matters, deadlines change), d (health: ask a subject-matter expert or check the package insert), e (quotes are readily invented), f (studies can be invented), h (the extension may not exist at all). What matters is your reasoning.

**Task 2:** The results differ from service to service. Typical: With web search there are more real links; without web search, titles or links are sometimes invented. Even a real source can say something different from what the AI claims. The conclusion: Always open sources yourself.

**Task 3:** Possible observation: The first answer contains more details, the second admits gaps. If the AI gives many precise details about a very small place, suspicion is warranted. A service with web search may do better here. Verify claims in a reliable source.

**Task 4:**
- Year of founding: yes, official website or chronicle of the museum
- Closing day: yes, current website or phone call (changes)
- Admission price: yes, current website
- Study: yes, does it really exist? Find and read the original
- Quote: yes, check wording and source; otherwise don't use it

**Task 5:** Example: *low* (gift idea): briefly consider whether it fits. *medium* (travel tip): a second source or a website. *high* (deadline, dose, publication): original source or subject-matter expert.
